Common Safety Measures

These simple routines dramatically cut your risk of becoming a victim.

🔑

Guard Your Credentials

  • ✓
    Never share your PIN, OTP, CVV, or full card number — not even with "bank staff".
  • ✓
    Use strong, unique passwords (test yours below) and change them regularly.
  • ✓
    Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) everywhere available.
🛡

Watch Your Accounts

  • ✓
    Review statements and transaction alerts regularly.
  • ✓
    Turn on real-time SMS / push notifications for every transaction.
  • ✓
    Report unfamiliar activity to your bank immediately.
🔗

Be Careful Online

  • ✓
    Only use your bank's official app or site — check for "https" and the lock icon.
  • ✓
    Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking; use a secure network or VPN.
  • ✓
    Don't click suspicious links in emails, SMS, or social media.
🤖

Stay Alert to Scams

  • ✓
    Banks never ask for OTPs or passwords over a call — hang up.
  • ✓
    Verify any "bank" message via the official number on your card.
  • ✓
    Keep your device software and antivirus up to date.
